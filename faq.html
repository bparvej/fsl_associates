How can I get legal advice for land issues?

Land matters in Bangladesh require verification of CS, SA, RS Porcha and current deeds. You can book an appointment to have our experts review your documents.

What is the process for filing a divorce?

Under Muslim Law, a notice must be sent to the Mayor/Chairman and the other party. The divorce becomes effective after 90 days if reconciliation fails.

How long does a check dishonor case take?

After a legal notice is served and ignored, a case under the NI Act is filed. Typically, these cases can take 1-3 years depending on court schedules.

Can NRBs handle property matters from abroad?

Yes, through a registered Power of Attorney (PoA) attested by the Bangladesh Embassy. We specialize in representing NRBs for their local legal needs.
